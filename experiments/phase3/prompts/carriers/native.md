# Carrier: concise native Japanese

Use ordinary concise Japanese. Complete sentences or compact bullet-like clauses are allowed, but do not use a custom grammar or fixed field layout.

Keep all decision-relevant negatives, limits, exceptions, identifiers, option IDs, action IDs, concept IDs, source IDs, epistemic distinctions, evidence associations, ordering, and authority boundaries. Remove only politeness, filler, repetition, and non-operative commentary.
